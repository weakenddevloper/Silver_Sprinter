Morning run, no stopping

Three big cockroaches are chasing Gyanu. Jump the benches, bins and puddles. When he trips, the man at the back whacks the roaches with his stick, and every 50 m the whip man hits him.

Level 2: run as the bald man. The bearded man chases you and throws his arrow every 100 m. It knocks you down, then you get up and run again. Don’t let him catch you.
